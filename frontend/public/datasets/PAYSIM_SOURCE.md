# PaySim sample provenance

`paysim-transactions.csv` is a deterministic 15,000-row investigation slice of the PaySim
mobile-money fraud research dataset. It was generated by
`scripts/prepare_paysim.py` from the public CSV archive hosted by the
AMLGraphX-Paysim dataset repository.

- Original research: E. A. Lopez-Rojas, A. Elmir, and S. Axelsson, “PaySim: A
  Financial Mobile Money Simulator for Fraud Detection,” EMSS 2016.
- Dataset page: https://huggingface.co/datasets/LordNR/AMLGraphX-Paysim
- License stated by the dataset repository: CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Rows retain PaySim sender, receiver, amount, and hour relationships.
- The slice contains all 8,197 positive-amount source-labelled fraud rows and
  6,803 of the earliest legitimate rows so a compact demo retains rare
  suspicious flows. Sixteen zero-amount source rows are excluded because the
  MuleTrace import contract requires positive transfer amounts.
- `paysim-source-labels.csv` preserves the source transaction type and labels;
  MuleTrace does not feed those labels to its rule detectors.
- Timestamps and transaction IDs are deterministic adapter fields required by
  the MuleTrace import contract. The displayed currency is INR for this demo;
  PaySim amounts are otherwise unitless.

PaySim is synthetic research data calibrated from real mobile-money behavior.
It is not a record of identifiable bank customers.
